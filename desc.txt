Core Requirements (What to Build):

Centralized EHR System:
A secure, digital database for all patient records, accessible by authorized users (doctors, nurses, admins) across hospitals and clinics.
Role-Based Access & Authentication:
Users log in securely; permissions are based on their role (e.g., doctor, nurse, admin, patient).
Patient Record Management:
Create, view, update, and search patient records (demographics, history, diagnoses, allergies, treatments, lab results).
Appointment Scheduling:
Patients and staff can book, reschedule, and cancel appointments; system prevents double-booking.
Prescription Management:
Doctors prescribe medications digitally; system checks for conflicts/allergies using AI.
AI Decision Support:
Alerts for prescription conflicts, allergies, and possibly other safety issues.
Automated Notifications:
Reminders for appointments, alerts for new lab results, etc.
Reporting & Analytics:
Dashboards for admins to view system usage, wait times, and other metrics.
Audit Logging:
Track all access and changes to records for security/compliance.
APIs for Interoperability:
Allow integration with other health systems (labs, pharmacies).
Non-Functional Requirements (How it Should Work):

Fast (load records in <2s), secure (encryption, RBAC), reliable (high uptime), scalable, easy to use, and standards-based (e.g., FHIR for data).
What’s NOT Required (for MVP):

Telemedicine, advanced disease prediction, or international data exchange—these are future goals.
What to Build First (MVP):

User authentication and role management
Patient record CRUD (create/read/update/search)
Appointment scheduling
Digital prescription with basic AI conflict check
Simple dashboard for admin
Audit log
